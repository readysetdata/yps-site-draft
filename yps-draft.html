How it works

From schematic to delivery in four steps

YPS handles the full lifecycle of a parts order so nothing falls through the cracks.

1

Browse schematics

Retailers open the parts schematic and see every component laid out visually — no part number lookup required.

2

Click to order

Click a marker on the schematic, confirm the part, and add it to your cart. Combine parts from multiple vendors in a single order.

3

Vendors fulfill

A single order can span multiple vendors. YPS routes each line to the right vendor, and each vendor manages status, fulfillment exceptions, and lead times in their own dashboard.

4

Analytics close the loop

Fill rate, lead time, and exception data flow into dashboards for both retailers and the manufacturer.

Platform features

Everything the parts process needs

YPS is a full-stack replacement parts infrastructure platform, not a point solution.

Interactive schematic builder

Build and publish parts schematics with clickable markers. Retailers place orders directly from the schematic with no part number lookup required.

Multi-tenant retailer portal

Every retailer gets their own scoped experience — order history, cart, and status visibility — across as many vendors and product lines as needed.

Intelligence dashboards

KPI cards, trend charts, and fill rate tables for vendors and retailers. Rolling 30-day windows with period-over-period comparisons built in.

Customer claims management

Track and manage direct customer claims end to end. Tie claims back to specific orders, parts, and vendors so nothing gets lost in email threads.

Parts inventory tracking

Maintain a live inventory system for your parts catalog. Know what's in stock, what's on order, and where shortfalls are before they affect retailers.

PDF reports

Periodic reports generated on demand. Downloadable, shareable, and always reflecting current order and fulfillment data.
